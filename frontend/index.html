What you’re looking at.

The club list and published Phase 1 fixtures come from the official Indian Super League website. Kickoff times are displayed in Indian Standard Time. Venues marked TBC have not been published in the imported schedule.

Completed results are added after verification against official match reports, or two independent reputable reports. Each result includes its sources. This app does not show live scores.

The points table is calculated from verified league-stage FT results using 3 points for a win and 1 for a draw. Equal-point clubs are displayed by goal difference, goals scored and name. Official head-to-head tie-breakers, deductions and rulings can change the official order.

Missing statistics stay unavailable. Published xG is shown only when explicitly sourced. No estimated shot locations or fictional player records are included.
