YOUR DATA, YOUR ANALYSIS

Bring the game to FotWeb.

No live feed is connected. The bundled dataset is synthetic: results, events and player statistics are illustrative, not official ISL records. Your uploads stay in this browser and can be removed at any time.

Use your own dataset

Download the example JSON, keep its structure, and replace the teams, matches and players with your data. Match shots use a 105 × 68 metre pitch; shot size is the supplied expected goals (xG).

Metrics explained

xG: expected goals values supplied with each shot. FotWeb sums these; it does not estimate xG. Per 90: stat ÷ minutes × 90. Shot conversion: goals ÷ shots. Similarity: cosine similarity of per-90 shooting, creation, passing and defensive metrics. Radar values are scaled to the largest value in the loaded player pool and are not league percentiles. Standings use 3 points for a win, then goal difference and goals scored as tie-breakers.
